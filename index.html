Welcome to Today Tool – your minimal ADHD-friendly task manager.

Features:

  • Today, Next, Someday, Archive, and New task cards
  • Master switch to show/hide all cards at once
  • Individual hide per card
  • Highlight important tasks with ⭐
  • Move tasks between cards using T/N/S/✕ buttons
  • New card creation and navigation (Prev/Next)
  • Export/Import Archive tasks as .txt
  • Scrollbars for task overflow

All tasks are saved automatically in your browser. Minimal layout keeps focus on tasks.
